Why does state_dict() exist?

To provide a portable representation of a model's persistent state.

What does it contain?
All registered Parameters.
All registered Buffers.
What does it not contain?
Python methods.
forward().
Class definitions.
Gradients.
Optimizer state.
Temporary runtime information.
Why a dictionary?

Because each tensor needs a stable, meaningful name based on the module hierarchy.

Key insight

The keys in state_dict() are not invented.

They come directly from the names established by the nn.Module hierarchy that you've already learned (named_parameters() and named_buffers()).
