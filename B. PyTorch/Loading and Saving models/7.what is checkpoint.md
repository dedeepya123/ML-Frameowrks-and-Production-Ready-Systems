What is a checkpoint?

A checkpoint is a snapshot of the training state, not just the model.

Typical contents
Model state_dict()
Optimizer state_dict()
Scheduler state_dict() (if used)
Current epoch
Global step
Optional: AMP scaler, RNG state, metrics, metadata
Why save a checkpoint?

To resume training from the exact point where it stopped.

Inference vs Training

Inference:

Model weights only

Resume Training:

Entire training state
Engineer's Thinking

When someone says:

"Save a checkpoint."

Your brain should automatically think:

"We're preserving the complete training progress so we can continue later—not just the learned weights."
