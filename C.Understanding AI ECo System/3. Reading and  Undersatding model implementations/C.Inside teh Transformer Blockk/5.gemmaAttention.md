# Module 4 — Inside the Transformer Block

## Lesson 5 — GemmaAttention

### Purpose

GemmaAttention is the module responsible for multi-head self-attention inside a Gemma decoder layer.

Its high-level responsibility is:

Take hidden states and allow information to flow between sequence positions through self-attention.

Input:
[B, S, H]

Output:
[B, S, H]

---

## 1. Why is attention a separate class?

GemmaDecoderLayer should not contain all attention implementation details.

Instead:

GemmaDecoderLayer
    └── GemmaAttention

The decoder layer delegates attention computation to GemmaAttention.

This is composition and separation of responsibility.

---

## 2. Main components

Conceptually:

GemmaAttention
├── q_proj
├── k_proj
├── v_proj
├── rotary_emb
└── o_proj

Additional configuration/cache/implementation details may also exist depending on the exact model version.

---

## 3. q_proj

Creates query representations.

Conceptually:

Q = XWq + bq

Input:
[B, S, H]

Output:
typically related to:
[B, S, num_heads × head_dim]

Meaning:

Queries represent what each token is looking for when deciding what information is relevant.

---

## 4. k_proj

Creates key representations.

K = XWk + bk

Keys are compared with queries to determine attention relevance.

---

## 5. v_proj

Creates value representations.

V = XWv + bv

Values contain the information that will be aggregated according to the attention weights.

---

## 6. q/k/v are separate learned projections

The same hidden state X is projected three different ways:

X
├── q_proj → Q
├── k_proj → K
└── v_proj → V

This allows the model to learn separate representations for querying, matching, and retrieving information.

---

## 7. Multi-head attention

Suppose:

H = 3072
num_heads = 24
head_dim = 128

because:

24 × 128 = 3072

Initial hidden representation:

[B, S, 3072]

After projection:

Q/K/V:
[B, S, 3072]

Reshape into heads:

[B, S, 24, 128]

Typically transpose to:

[B, 24, S, 128]

where:

B = batch
24 = attention heads
S = sequence length
128 = dimension per head

The heads are represented as a tensor dimension.

They do not need to be separate Python objects.

---

## 8. Why multiple heads?

Multi-head attention allows attention to operate in multiple learned representation subspaces.

Conceptually:

Head 1 → one learned relationship pattern
Head 2 → another learned relationship pattern
...
Head N → another relationship pattern

Specific semantic specialization of individual heads should not be assumed.

---

## 9. Attention is self-attention

Q, K and V all originate from the same hidden states:

X
├── Q projection → Q
├── K projection → K
└── V projection → V

Therefore it is self-attention.

In cross-attention, Q and K/V would originate from different representations.

---

## 10. rotary_emb

RoPE provides positional information to the attention mechanism.

Conceptually:

Q → RoPE → Q'
K → RoPE → K'
V → unchanged

RoPE is not a Q/K/V projection.

Detailed RoPE behavior will be studied in a separate lesson.

---

## 11. o_proj

After each attention head produces its output:

head 1 ─┐
head 2 ─┤
...     ├→ combine → o_proj
head N ─┘

The result is returned to the model hidden representation:

[B, S, H]

o_proj is therefore the projection that maps the combined attention output back into the model's hidden space.

---

## 12. High-level attention pipeline

hidden_states
    [B,S,H]
       │
       ├── q_proj → Q
       ├── k_proj → K
       └── v_proj → V
               ↓
          reshape into heads
               ↓
       [B, heads, S, head_dim]
               ↓
           RoPE on Q/K
               ↓
         attention computation
               ↓
       [B, heads, S, head_dim]
               ↓
          combine heads
               ↓
            [B,S,H]
               ↓
             o_proj
               ↓
            [B,S,H]

---

## 13. Important code-reading principle

When seeing:

hidden_states = self.self_attn(hidden_states)

do not treat this as one opaque operation.

Expand it mentally:

GemmaDecoderLayer
    ↓
GemmaAttention
    ├── Q projection
    ├── K projection
    ├── V projection
    ├── positional processing
    ├── attention computation
    └── output projection

One Python call can represent an entire computation graph.

---

## 14. Object hierarchy

GemmaDecoderLayer
    │
    └── GemmaAttention
          ├── q_proj
          ├── k_proj
          ├── v_proj
          ├── rotary_emb
          └── o_proj

Each class owns one level of responsibility.

---

## 15. Core mental model

GemmaAttention:

hidden states
    ↓
create Q/K/V
    ↓
split into multiple heads
    ↓
add positional information to Q/K
    ↓
calculate attention
    ↓
aggregate V
    ↓
combine heads
    ↓
output projection
    ↓
hidden states

The next lessons will open up each of these stages.
