# Module 1 – Lesson 3

## Categories of AI Frameworks

### 1. Model Libraries
Purpose:
- Provide model architectures.
- Provide pretrained weights.
- Standardize implementations.

Examples:
- Hugging Face Transformers
- torchvision
- timm

---

### 2. Training Frameworks
Purpose:
- Simplify or scale model training.

Examples:
- PyTorch Lightning
- DeepSpeed
- Accelerate

---

### 3. Fine-tuning Frameworks
Purpose:
- Efficiently adapt pretrained models.

Examples:
- PEFT
- LoRA libraries

---

### 4. Inference Frameworks
Purpose:
- Run trained models efficiently.

Examples:
- vLLM
- TensorRT-LLM
- ONNX Runtime
- QAIRT

---

### 5. Model Hubs
Purpose:
- Store and share pretrained models.

Example:
- Hugging Face Hub

---

### 6. Evaluation Frameworks
Purpose:
- Measure model quality.

---

### 7. Experiment Tracking
Purpose:
- Track training runs, metrics, and checkpoints.

## Learning Rule

Every AI framework exists to solve a specific engineering problem.

When learning a new framework, first identify its category before learning its APIs.
