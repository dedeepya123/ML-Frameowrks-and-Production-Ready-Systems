# Module 6 — Lesson 1: What Happens to a Linear Layer?

## Normal Linear

Y = XW + b

At model level:
nn.Linear(...)

Conceptually:
activation × weight

---

## Weight Quantization

W ≈ sw × Wq

where:
- Wq = integer/low-precision weight
- sw = weight scale

Therefore:

Y = XW

becomes approximately:

Y ≈ sw × (XWq)

---

## Weight + Activation Quantization

X ≈ sx × Xq
W ≈ sw × Wq

Therefore:

Y ≈ sx × sw × (XqWq)

This explains why quantization scales are required.

---

## Important Distinction

Model-level operation:

Linear

Tensor-level operation:

Xq × Wq

Kernel-level operation:

quantized GEMM

Hardware-level execution:

NPU matrix/integer compute unit

---

## Always Distinguish

Storage dtype
↓
Compute dtype
↓
Accumulator dtype
↓
Output dtype

They do not necessarily have to be identical.

---

## Production Reasoning

When reading quantized runtime code ask:

1. What does model code represent?
2. What datatype is stored?
3. What operator does it become?
4. What datatype does the kernel consume?
5. What is the accumulator?
6. Where are scales applied?
7. Which kernel executes?
8. What does the target NPU actually run?

## Core Mental Model

Model
→ Operator
